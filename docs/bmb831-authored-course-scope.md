# BMB831 authored-course scope

The Study Hub now contains a nine-module BMB831 preparation sequence aligned with the active public SDU course description.

## Included academic scope

1. Relational and longitudinal data contracts.
2. Omics matrix quality control and normalisation.
3. Differential modelling and multiplicity control.
4. Multivariate analysis and stability.
5. Advanced visualisation and reproducible export.
6. Versioned public transcriptomics and proteomics workflow contracts.
7. Computational protein characterisation.
8. Biological interpretation, enrichment, pathways, and networks.
9. Publication appraisal and the individual English report.

## Included learning evidence

- strict Spanish, English, and Danish content;
- deterministic editable R laboratories;
- guided individual practice;
- integrated assessment and stable objective banks;
- public-source provenance contracts;
- persistent learner-owned English report drafts.

## Institutional boundary

The application prepares the academic content and individual report workflow. It does not certify official tutorial attendance, reproduce private Itslearning exercises or grading rubrics, or assign an official SDU grade.
